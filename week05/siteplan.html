Summoner's Index

Student: Andrei Enos Flores Villapaz

1. Site Name

Summoner's Index

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Summoner's Index is to provide a simple, fast, and visually appealing reference guide to the champions of League of Legends. It is designed to help players quickly browse and identify all champions without the overwhelming statistics found on other platforms.

3. Scenarios

  • What is a quick way to look up a champion's art and title after a match?
  • How can I keep track of champions that my friends recommend I should try?

4. Color Schema

  • Primary/Background: Dark Blue (#0a1018)
  • Accent/Highlight: Gold (#c89b3c)
  • Text: Off-White (#f0e6d2)

5. Typography

  • Headings Font: Cinzel
  • Body Font: Roboto
